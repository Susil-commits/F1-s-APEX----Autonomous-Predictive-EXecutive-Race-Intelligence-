# FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations — Article 30.5: Tyre Compound Allocation & Wet Weather Conditions

## Overview
Regulations governing mandatory tyre compound usage during Grands Prix and the operational shifts triggered by precipitation forecasts or wet weather transitions.

## Relevant Strategy Levers
- `weather_transition_flag`: Forecasted mid-race dry-to-wet or wet-to-dry transition risk.
- `race_rain_prob`: Pre-race precipitation probability at race start.

## Key Regulatory Clauses
1. **Article 30.5(n) (Two Dry Compound Mandate)**: Unless the race is declared wet by the Race Director, each competitor must use at least two different specifications of dry-weather tyres (slick compounds) during the race.
2. **Article 30.5(o) (Wet Weather Compound Exemption)**: If the race track is officially declared wet by the Race Director, or if the driver fits intermediate or full wet weather tyres at any point during the Grand Prix, the requirement to run two distinct dry slick compounds is automatically waived.
3. **Article 30.2 (Extreme Wet Start Requirement)**: If a race starts behind the Safety Car due to heavy precipitation or standing water, the use of Extreme Wet tyres is mandatory for all cars until the Safety Car withdraws.
4. **Article 30.3 (Grid Start Tire Blankets)**: Operating temperatures of tyre heating blankets prior to grid departure must not exceed 70°C for slicks and 60°C for wet weather compounds to prevent thermal degradation before lights out.
