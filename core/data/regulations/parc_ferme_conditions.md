# FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations — Article 40: Parc Fermé Conditions

## Overview
From the moment a competitor's car commences qualifying practice (Q1) until the start of the race formation lap, the car is deemed to be in Parc Fermé conditions.

## Relevant Strategy Levers
- `quali_delta_to_pole_s`: Qualifying pace delta to pole position.
- `grid_position_norm`: Grid starting position.
- `quali_gap`: Qualifying delta to the front row.

## Key Regulatory Clauses
1. **Article 40.1 (Setup Restrictions)**: No setup modifications, aerodynamic configuration changes, suspension geometry adjustments, or spring/damper rate alterations may be carried out once qualifying begins.
2. **Article 40.2 (Permitted Grid Adjustments)**: The only aerodynamic adjustment permitted on the starting grid prior to the formation lap is the physical angle alteration of the front wing flap elements using the existing adjuster mechanism, within limits defined by Technical Regulations.
3. **Article 40.8 (Pit Lane Start Penalty)**: Any change made to the suspension geometry, power unit specification, or aerodynamic surface setup outside of permitted routine maintenance incurs a mandatory penalty requiring the driver to start the race from the pit lane.
4. **Article 39.4 (107% Qualifying Rule)**: Any driver whose best qualifying lap time in Q1 exceeds 107% of the fastest time set in that session will not be classified or allowed to participate in the race without specific dispensation granted by the FIA Stewards based on suitable free practice lap times.
